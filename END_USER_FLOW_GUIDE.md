# Job Portal - End User Flow Guide

## 🎯 Overview

This guide explains how employers and administrators use the Job Portal system to request job seekers, make payments, and manage the entire hiring process from start to finish.

## 👥 User Types

### 1. **Employers** - Companies looking to hire

### 2. **Administrators** - System managers who oversee the process

### 3. **Job Seekers** - Candidates available for hire

---

## 🚀 **Complete Workflow for Employers**

### **Step 1: Initial Request Submission**

#### What Happens:

1. **Visit the Job Portal** website
2. **Fill out the request form** with your details:
   - Your name and company information
   - Contact details (phone, email)
   - Job requirements and message
   - Specific candidate (if you have one in mind)
   - Priority level (low, normal, high, urgent)

#### What You Get:

- **Confirmation email** with your request details
- **Login credentials** (if this is your first time):
  - Email address
  - Generated password (e.g., `abc@123`)
  - Dashboard access instructions

#### Example Request:

```
Company: Tech Solutions Ltd
Looking for: Skilled JavaScript developer
Requirements: 3+ years experience, React, Node.js
Priority: High
Message: "Need developer for urgent project starting next week"
```

---

### **Step 2: Account Creation & Dashboard Access**

#### First-Time Users:

1. **Save your login credentials** from the email
2. **Visit the login page**
3. **Enter your email and password**
4. **Access your employer dashboard**

#### Dashboard Features:

- **Request Overview**: See all your requests
- **Status Tracking**: Monitor request progress
- **Payment Management**: Handle payment requests
- **Candidate Access**: View job seeker details (when granted)

---

### **Step 3: Payment Request Process**

#### What Happens:

1. **Admin reviews your request**
2. **Admin sends payment request** for:
   - **Photo Access** (5000 RWF) - See candidate's photo
   - **Full Details** (Higher amount) - Complete contact information

#### Payment Request Email:

```
Subject: Payment Request - Photo Access - Job Portal

Hello John Doe,

Please complete payment of 5000 RWF for photo access.

Account: Job Portal
Number: 0788123456
Amount: 5000 RWF

Next Steps:
1. Transfer 5000 RWF to the account above
2. Confirm your payment with your name and phone number
3. Admin will review and approve your payment
4. Photo access will be granted automatically after approval
```

---

### **Step 4: Making the Payment**

#### Payment Options Available:

- **MTN MOMO** (Mobile Money)
- **Airtel Money** (Mobile Money)
- **Bank Transfer** (if configured)

#### Payment Process:

1. **Transfer the requested amount** to the provided account
2. **Save your payment details**:
   - Transfer reference number
   - Transfer date and time
   - Amount transferred

---

### **Step 5: Payment Confirmation**

#### Confirm Your Payment:

1. **Go to your dashboard**
2. **Click "Confirm Payment"** for the specific request
3. **Fill in the confirmation form**:
   - Your name (as shown on transfer)
   - Your phone number
   - Payment reference number
   - Transfer amount
   - Transfer date
   - Additional notes (optional)

#### Confirmation Form:

```
Payment Confirmation Form
├── Your Name: [John Doe]
├── Phone Number: [0788123456]
├── Payment Reference: [MTN123456]
├── Transfer Amount: [5000 RWF]
├── Transfer Date: [2024-01-15]
└── Notes: [Payment made via MTN MOMO]
```

#### What Happens Next:

- **Payment status changes** to "Confirmed"
- **Admin receives notification** to review your payment
- **Progress tracking updated** in your dashboard

---

### **Step 6: Admin Review & Approval**

#### Admin Actions:

1. **Reviews your payment confirmation**
2. **Verifies payment details**
3. **Approves or rejects** the payment
4. **Grants access** to candidate information

#### Possible Outcomes:

- ✅ **Payment Approved**: Access granted immediately
- ❌ **Payment Rejected**: Admin provides reason, you may need to re-pay
- ⏳ **More Info Needed**: Admin requests additional details

---

### **Step 7: Access Granted**

#### Photo Access Granted:

- **Candidate's photo** becomes visible
- **Basic information** remains anonymized
- **Contact details** still hidden
- **Next step**: Request full details access

#### Full Details Access Granted:

- **Complete candidate information** available
- **Contact details** (phone, email)
- **Full profile** with all skills and experience
- **Direct communication** possible

---

### **Step 8: Candidate Selection & Hiring**

#### After Access Granted:

1. **Review candidate details** thoroughly
2. **Assess skills and experience**
3. **Make hiring decision**
4. **Contact candidate directly** (if full access granted)
5. **Proceed with hiring process**

## 🚨 **Common Scenarios & Solutions**

### **Scenario 1: Payment Not Showing as Confirmed**

#### **Possible Causes**:

- Payment confirmation not submitted
- Admin hasn't reviewed yet
- Payment details don't match

#### **Solutions**:

1. **Check dashboard** for payment status
2. **Submit payment confirmation** if not done
3. **Contact admin** if confirmation submitted but not showing
4. **Verify payment details** match exactly

### **Scenario 2: Access Not Granted After Payment**

#### **Possible Causes**:

- Payment still under review
- Payment rejected by admin
- Technical system issue

#### **Solutions**:

1. **Wait for admin review** (usually within 24 hours)
2. **Check email** for admin notifications
3. **Contact support** if issue persists
4. **Verify payment was approved** in dashboard

### **Scenario 3: Need to Update Request Details**

#### **Possible Causes**:

- Information changed after submission
- Additional requirements added
- Priority level needs adjustment

#### **Solutions**:

1. **Contact admin** to update request
2. **Provide new information** via email
3. **Request priority change** if urgent
4. **Submit new request** if major changes needed
